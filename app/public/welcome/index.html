The protocol

What travels with the funds?

Seal at the source

The sender’s browser encrypts the record to the receiving exchange’s registered key and a separate key for the quarter’s auditor.

Check the deposit

The receiver rebuilds the transfer details from the deposit. Changing the amount breaks decryption. The receiver signs its decision on chain.

Audit one quarter

The auditor reconciles records for its quarter. Destroying that key removes the auditor’s access. The receiving exchange retains its own copy.
